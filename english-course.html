Final Reflection

How the course connects to my future career.

The Work Ready Now course helped me understand that employability is not only about having technical skills. It is also about knowing how to present those skills clearly, professionally, and confidently. Through CV writing, portfolio preparation, and workplace-readiness activities, I learned how to connect my academic background and work experiences to real professional strengths.

The course also helped me reflect on my future as a Mechanical Engineering student who wants to become an automotive engineer. I now understand that communication, organization, and professionalism are important parts of engineering work. Building this portfolio allowed me to present my experiences, goals, and values in a structured way, and it helped me become more prepared for future internships and career opportunities.

Connection to Automotive Engineering

Automotive engineering requires more than technical knowledge. It also requires teamwork, documentation, problem-solving, safety awareness, and the ability to communicate ideas clearly with engineers, managers, and clients.
