Course Purpose

Preparing for the workplace through communication and reflection.

The Work Ready Now English course helped me prepare for professional environments by improving how I present myself, organize my achievements, and communicate my skills. The course focused on career readiness, CV writing, portfolio creation, workplace communication, and professional self-presentation.

Professional Communication

I learned how to communicate ideas more clearly and professionally, especially when describing my experience, goals, and strengths.

Career Preparation

I practiced preparing documents and materials that are useful for internships, interviews, applications, and future professional opportunities.

Portfolio Building

Connection to My Future Career

Why English and communication matter in engineering.

Automotive engineering requires more than technical knowledge. Engineers must communicate clearly, work in teams, document ideas, explain problems, and present solutions. This course helped me understand that communication, organization, and professionalism are important parts of engineering work. By improving my CV, portfolio, and career reflection, I became more prepared for future internships and professional opportunities.

Final Reflection

What the Work Ready Now course taught me.

The Work Ready Now course helped me understand that employability is not only about having technical skills. It is also about knowing how to present those skills clearly, professionally, and confidently. Through CV writing, portfolio preparation, and workplace-readiness activities, I learned how to connect my academic background and work experiences to real professional strengths.

The course also helped me reflect on my future as a Mechanical Engineering student who wants to become an automotive engineer. I now understand that communication, organization, and professionalism are important parts of engineering work.

Building this portfolio allowed me to present my experiences, goals, and values in a structured way, and it helped me become more prepared for future internships and career opportunities.
