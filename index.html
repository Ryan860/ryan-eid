Career Summary

A motivated engineering student with practical work experience.

Ryan Eid is a Mechanical Engineering student at Notre Dame University – Louaize with a growing technical foundation in SolidWorks, MATLAB, Microsoft Excel, and basic Arduino/electronics.

He is a versatile and disciplined student with experience in safety, instruction, operations, events, and customer service.

Reflections

Connecting course learning, work experience, and future goals.

Work-Based Learning Reflection

My work-based learning experiences helped me understand how responsibility, communication, and adaptability are used in real professional environments. The experience that shaped me the most was working as a runner with ODIN Events during Summer 2024. In that role, I coordinated logistics, moved between teams, communicated information quickly, and responded to last-minute requests. This taught me how to stay organized and calm while working under pressure.

My lifeguard experience developed my sense of responsibility because safety requires focus, discipline, and quick decision-making. As a junior ski instructor, I learned how to communicate patiently, support beginners, and maintain safety while helping others improve. Managing a kiosk at Crêpe Factory gave me early exposure to operations, customer service, supervision, and inventory management. My long-term involvement in the Boy Scouts also developed leadership, teamwork, discipline, and service.

Together, these experiences helped me confirm that I want a career where technical knowledge and practical responsibility meet. Automotive engineering interests me because it requires precision, problem-solving, teamwork, and real-world application. My work exposure showed me that professional success depends not only on technical skills, but also on communication, reliability, discipline, and the ability to perform under pressure.

English Course Reflection — Work Ready Now

The Work Ready Now course helped me understand that employability is not only about having technical skills. It is also about knowing how to present those skills clearly, professionally, and confidently. Through CV writing, portfolio preparation, and workplace-readiness activities, I learned how to connect my academic background and work experiences to real professional strengths. The course also helped me reflect on my future as a Mechanical Engineering student who wants to become an automotive engineer.
